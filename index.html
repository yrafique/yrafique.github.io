Signals

The market’s unusual activity, ranked.

Volume, price and flow are scored together, so the stocks worth a look rise to the top. Open any one and the number you saw in the list is the number on the page.

  • One score per stock, the same everywhere
  • Pre-market and after-hours moves shown separately from the close
  • Every score explained, never a bare verdict

  • Inflows in green, outflows in bronze, always with an arrow

Research desk

Ask in plain English.

Ask about a stock, a sector or the day. The assistant answers from the app’s own data, shows the time it was updated, and cites the publisher of any headline it uses. It does research, not advice.

  • Charts drawn from the same data you see elsewhere in the app
  • Turns off completely if you prefer

Honest by design

Numbers you can trace.

No made-up figures. When something is missing, the app says so instead of guessing.

Sources

Named, always

Every headline shows its publisher and links to their page.

Time

Stamped, always

Every figure shows when it was last updated, so you know how fresh it is.

Delay

Straight about it

Prices may be delayed up to about 15 minutes. Economic data is end-of-day. Nothing is passed off as real-time.

Privacy

Private by default.

No account, no ads, no tracking. Your holdings stay on your device, and in your own iCloud if you turn sync on. The AI assistant only sees your portfolio if you switch that on, and you can switch it off at any time.

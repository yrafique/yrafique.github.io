A tour of the app

Five screens. One quiet instrument.

01 Signals

Unusual activity, ranked.

Volume, price and flow are scored together, so the stocks worth a closer look rise to the top.

  • One score per stock, the same number on every screen
  • Pre-market and after-hours moves kept apart from the close
  • Every score explained, never a bare verdict

02 Flows

See where the money moved.

Net flow by sector over the period you choose, from one day to five years. Axes carry their units, and every figure matches the period on screen.

  • Six periods, one tap each. Try them on the phone
  • Inflows in green, outflows in bronze, always with an arrow

03 News

Every headline names its source.

Market news from about 50 publishers, newest first. Each item shows who published it and when, and opens the publisher’s own page.

  • Publisher and time on every headline
  • Subscription sources are marked before you tap
  • Public filings sit alongside, clearly labelled

04 Research desk

Ask in plain English.

Ask about a stock, a sector or the day. The assistant answers from the data the app has loaded, stamps when it was updated and cites the publisher of any headline it uses.

  • Shows its sources and its time
  • Research, not advice
  • Turns off completely if you prefer

05 Portfolio

Your holdings stay on your iPhone.

Track what you own with risk measures that state their period. Nothing is uploaded to us, and there is no account.

  • Stored on your device, or your own iCloud if you turn sync on
  • AI review is opt-in, and off by default
  • Every risk figure labelled with its period

Honest by design

Numbers you can trace.

No made-up figures and no quiet guesses. Every number in the app tells you where it came from and when.

  • Named sources. Every headline shows its publisher and links to their page.
  • Stamped times. Every figure shows when it was last updated.
  • Straight about delay. Prices may be delayed up to about 15 minutes. Economic data is end-of-day.
  • Missing means missing. When a figure isn’t available, the app says so instead of guessing.

Private by default

Your money is your business.

No account, no ads, no tracking. Your holdings stay on your iPhone, and in your own iCloud if you turn sync on. The AI assistant sees your portfolio only if you switch that on, and you can switch it off at any time.

Questions

Good to know.

Anything else? Visit support.

Is the data real-time?

No. Prices may be delayed up to about 15 minutes, and economic data is end-of-day. Every screen shows the time its data was last updated, so you always know how fresh it is.

Where does the data come from?

Market data, public regulatory filings and economic data come from third-party providers. Every headline names its publisher and opens the publisher’s own page. Economic data from the Federal Reserve is credited to it.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-up and no account. Open the app and start reading.

Is my portfolio private?

Yes. Holdings stay on your iPhone, and in your own iCloud if you turn sync on. We don’t receive them. The AI assistant only sees your portfolio if you switch on portfolio sharing in Settings.

How does the AI research assistant work?

It is an AI language model that answers from the market data and headlines the app has already loaded. Some analysis runs on your device; otherwise your question and the relevant market context are sent over an encrypted connection to an AI service to write the answer. It shows when its data was updated and which publishers it used. It can be wrong, and you can turn it off.

Is this investment advice?

No. MoneyFlows is a research tool for information and education. Scores, signals and AI answers are not recommendations. Do your own research and consider a licensed professional.

Which devices does it run on?

MoneyFlows is an iPhone app. The App Store page lists the iOS version it needs.

How do I contact support?

Email moneyflowapp@proton.me. Include your iPhone model, iOS version and app version, and we’ll get back to you.

Common questions

Is the data real-time?

No. Prices may be delayed by up to about 15 minutes, and economic indicators update once a day at most. Each screen shows the time its data was last updated.

Where do the numbers and news come from?

Market data, public regulatory filings and economic data come from third-party providers. Every news headline shows the publisher it came from and opens the publisher's own page. Economic data from the Federal Reserve is credited to the Federal Reserve.

How do I turn AI sharing off?

The AI assistant only sees your portfolio if you turn on Share portfolio with AI assistant in Settings (About, privacy & legal). It is off by default. You can switch it off at any time.

How do I delete my data?

Holdings and watchlists are stored on your device (and in your own iCloud if you turn sync on). Deleting the app removes the local data. There is no account to delete.

Is this investment advice?

No. Money Flow is for information and education only. Scores, signals and AI answers are not recommendations. Do your own research and consider speaking to a licensed financial adviser before making investment decisions.

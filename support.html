Common questions

Is the data real-time?

No. Prices may be delayed up to about 15 minutes, and economic data is end-of-day. Every screen shows the time its data was last updated, so you always know how fresh it is.

Where do the numbers and news come from?

Market data, public regulatory filings and economic data come from third-party providers. Every headline names its publisher and opens the publisher’s own page. Economic data from the Federal Reserve is credited to it.

How do I turn AI portfolio sharing off?

The AI assistant only sees your portfolio if you turn on Share portfolio with AI assistant in Settings (About, privacy & legal). It is off by default, and you can switch it off at any time.

How do I delete my data?

Holdings and watchlists are stored on your device, and in your own iCloud if you turn sync on. Removing a holding deletes it immediately, and deleting the app removes all local data. There is no account to delete.

A number looks wrong or missing. What should I do?

Check the time stamp first: prices may be delayed up to about 15 minutes, and some figures update once a day. If a figure is unavailable the app shows it as missing rather than guessing. If something still looks off, email us with the screen and the stock.

How does the AI research assistant work?

It is an AI language model that answers from the market data and headlines the app has already loaded. Some analysis runs on your device; otherwise your question and the relevant market context are sent over an encrypted connection to an AI service to write the answer. It shows when its data was updated and which publishers it used. It can be wrong, and you can turn it off.

Is this investment advice?

No. MoneyFlows is a research tool for information and education. Scores, signals and AI answers are not recommendations. Do your own research and consider a licensed professional.

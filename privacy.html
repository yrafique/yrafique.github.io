Privacy Policy

Money Flow — Last updated: November 4, 2026

Summary

  • No account or sign-up. We don't know who you are.
  • We don't run servers that store your data. Your portfolio lives on your device and, if you turn it on, in your own iCloud.
  • No ads, no analytics SDKs, no tracking, and we don't sell data.
  • The app does contact data providers, news sources and, for AI answers, an AI provider. Details below.
  • Prices are 15-minute delayed and economic data is end-of-day. Nothing here is real-time.

What You Enter

Your portfolio holdings (tickers, shares, cost basis) and watchlist are stored on your device. If you enable iCloud, they sync through your iCloud account under Apple's terms. We do not receive them.

Questions you type or speak into the AI research desk are sent to an AI provider to get an answer (see AI Features).

What We Do Not Collect

  • No name, email, contacts, precise location or photos
  • No advertising identifiers
  • No usage analytics or crash-reporting SDKs

Data Providers, News and Media

The app fetches public market, economic and news data directly from third-party services. Every request exposes your IP address and the app's user agent to the service that receives it. Each provider's own privacy policy governs how it handles that.

  • Market data providers — prices (may be delayed, up to about 15 minutes), company data and per-stock news. Receive the stock symbol you view.
  • Public regulatory filings — insider (Form 4), institutional (13F) and other filings. Receive the stock symbol when you open a stock.
  • Short-volume data — daily short-sale volume from fixed files. No symbol is sent.
  • Economic data providers — economic indicators, end-of-day. No symbol is sent.
  • News publishers — market-wide headlines from about 50 sources. These are fixed feed addresses, so they see only your IP address and user agent, not what you look at. News is credited to the publisher and author shown on each article. Tapping a headline opens the publisher's own site.
  • Embedded video — video cards load from a third-party video platform only when one is shown.

We do not control these third parties' practices.

AI Features

  • On supported devices, some analysis runs on-device with Apple's on-device intelligence. Then your question stays on the device.
  • Otherwise your question and relevant market context are sent over an encrypted connection to third-party AI service providers. We may change providers at any time.
  • Requests pass through a cloud relay service we operate. The relay forwards requests and does not log or store their content.
  • Portfolio sharing is off by default. Only if you turn on "Share portfolio with AI assistant" in Settings are your holdings (tickers, share counts, values, cash, profit/loss) included in AI chat and the portfolio review, and sent to the same providers. With it off, the portfolio review is generated on your device.
  • Don't enter sensitive personal information into the AI. Chat questions are not tied to any account.

Each AI provider's own privacy policy governs how it handles what it receives. AI output is informational only and may be inaccurate.

Voice Input

The microphone is used only when you tap the voice button. Audio is transcribed by Apple's Speech Recognition, which may use Apple's servers under Apple's policy. The text becomes your search query. We don't record or store audio.

Notifications

Alerts are local. The app checks your alert rules on your device while it is open. Nothing about them is sent to a server, and we run no push-notification server.

Storage and Deletion

Portfolio, watchlist and settings are stored on your device. Cached market data and AI-answer feedback are kept in local databases that stay on the device. Removing a holding deletes it immediately, and deleting the app removes all local data. iCloud copies are managed in your iCloud settings.

Security

Network requests use HTTPS/TLS. Because we have no accounts and no data-storing backend, there is no central store of your personal data.

Not Investment Advice

Money Flow is a research tool. It does not provide investment, financial, tax or legal advice. Data may be delayed or inaccurate, and AI output can be wrong. Do your own research and consider a licensed professional.

Children's Privacy

The app is for adults and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to This Policy

If we update this policy, the revised version will be posted here with an updated date.
